About

A B.Tech physics project about one question and an honest answer.

Method and data

Prices come from the characteristic-function formula and are checked against Monte Carlo simulation.

Research data: official NSE end-of-day files, 210 stocks, 60 trading days, 12,480 option surfaces. Live prices on the site come from Upstox.

Every real-market result was re-run after three pipeline bugs were found and fixed, and checked against a flat volatility, which a true best fit can never lose to.

Limits

  • No jumps: the largest one-day moves are still underestimated.
  • Dividends are supported but set to zero; one interest rate for every maturity.
  • Prices are frictionless. Real trading crosses a bid–ask spread.
  • End-of-day research data has closing prices only, no bid–ask quotes.
  • Only 11 to 19 of the 20 target option slots are quoted on a typical day.

Also explored

Also explored: whether EWMA or GARCH forecasts of next month's volatility beat simply assuming it looks like last month, tested walk-forward over 60 stocks and ten years.
