The model

Price a listed option with Double Heston and set it against the market. The starting settings assume 20% volatility. On 25 Sep 2026 the market was pricing about 11.4%, so the model charges more. Lower today's level (v₀) and the gap closes. There's no fit button: the finding explains why a computer can't pick all ten settings for you.
