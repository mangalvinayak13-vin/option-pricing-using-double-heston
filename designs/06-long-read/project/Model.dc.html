The model

Pricing one option, in public

Pick a listed option, price it with Double Heston, and set it against what the market paid.

A B.Tech physics project on the Double Heston model. Prices: NSE, close of 25 Sep 2026.

TypeCallPut

The NIFTY 23,150 call expiring 27 Oct 2026 closed at ₹370.15. Double Heston, at its starting settings, prices it at ₹600.40, a gap of ₹230.25. A Monte Carlo simulation of 20,000 paths gives ₹597.10 ± 3.56, so the formula and the simulation agree.

The starting settings assume 20% volatility. On 25 Sep 2026 the market was pricing about 11.4%, so the model charges more. Lower today's level (v₀) and the gap closes. There's no fit button: the finding explains why a computer can't pick all ten settings for you.
