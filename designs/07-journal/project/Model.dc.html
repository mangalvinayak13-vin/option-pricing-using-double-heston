3Pricing a listed option

We price the NIFTY 23,150 call expiring 27 Oct 2026 (32 days), which closed at ₹370.15 on 25 Sep 2026 (implied volatility 11.37%). At its starting settings Double Heston gives ₹600.40 (implied volatility 19.87%); a Monte Carlo check with 20,000 paths gives ₹597.10 ± 3.56.

The starting settings assume 20% volatility. On 25 Sep 2026 the market was pricing about 11.4%, so the model charges more. Lower today's level (v₀) and the gap closes. There's no fit button: the finding explains why a computer can't pick all ten settings for you.

Option prices: NSE closing prices, 25 Sep 2026. Forward from NIFTY October futures (23,263.60). Rate: RBI 91-day T-bill, 15 Jul observation carried forward, as in the research.
